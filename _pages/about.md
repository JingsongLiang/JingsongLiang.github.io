**Jingsong Liang is a PhD student in Robotics and AI at [Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/), supervised by [Prof. Chen Lv](https://lvchen.wixsite.com/automan) and [Prof. Wanhua Li](https://li-wanhua.github.io/). He is a recipient of the IGP Artificial Intelligence-X Scholarship.**

**His research centers on Multimodal Embodied Generalist Agents (MEGA), aiming to build general-purpose agents that integrate multimodal perception, embodied reasoning, autonomous planning, and continual self-improvement in the physical world. He also works closely with [Sharpa](https://www.sharpa.com/) on visual-tactile manipulation.**
